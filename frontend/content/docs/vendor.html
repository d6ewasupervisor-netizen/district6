Vendor Standards

Dress & Conduct Policy

Thanks for partnering with us. These standards keep our stores safe, professional, and welcoming for customers — and they apply to every vendor visit.

Conduct

Before You Start

  • Park in outlying lot areas. Enter through the back and sign the vendor log first thing.
  • Leave kids at home. Children are not permitted while you work, and only adults 18+ may operate backroom equipment such as the cardboard baler.

While You're On the Floor

  • Help customers when asked. If you can't answer, find a store associate — never reply with "I don't work here."
  • No pallets on the sales floor after the division-mandated cutoff. Use U-boats or appropriate vendor carts, and pull, don't push, for clear sightlines.
  • Shopping carts are for customers only. Don't use them to move or store product.

Reset team exception: SAS / Retail Odyssey has been granted exclusive rights by Kroger corporate to use shopping carts during resets. This is standard corporate policy, but expect occasional pushback from store employees who aren't aware of the exception. Two non-negotiables: never leave product in a cart in the backroom, and empty and return every cart to the corral before ending your shift.

  • No music devices, headphones, or earbuds — anywhere in the store, anytime.
  • Cell phones stay on vibrate. Use them only for business needs, and never while moving product on the floor.
  • No eating, drinking, or tobacco use (including smokeless) on store premises.
  • You are not authorized to operate powered forklifts.
  • Wipe down shelves and display areas weekly, or more often as needed.

Before You Leave

  • Don't leave trash or product on the floor. Distressed merchandise goes to the backroom for credit.
  • Combine like items on one pallet, stretch wrap for safety, and stage in the designated backroom area.

Heads up: Keep receipts for everything you purchase. Bags and briefcases may be searched. Suspect something off? Call the ethics hotline at 1-800-689-4609.

Dress Code

Show up clean, well-groomed, and identifiable. Wear a name badge that displays both your company name and your own name.

Shirts

✓ Acceptable
  • Polo shirts
  • Blouses or shirts with sleeves
  • Sweaters and cardigans
  • Anything that covers the midriff
✕ Not Acceptable
  • T-shirts and sweatshirts
  • Tank tops or spaghetti straps
  • Shirts with non–company/product graphics or text

Slacks & Skirts

✓ Acceptable
  • Casual slacks (e.g., Dockers) worn at the waist
  • Company-issue uniform shorts
  • Clean, well-kept jeans (if your company allows them)
✕ Not Acceptable
  • Sweatpants or exercise wear
  • Tight, cutoff, or baggy shorts
  • Anything shorter than 3 inches above the knee

Shoes

✓ Acceptable
  • Closed-toe casual or low-heel shoes
  • Boating or deck shoes
  • Steel-toe and athletic shoes
  • Loafers and dress shoes
✕ Not Acceptable
  • Casual sandals
  • Flip-flops
  • Any open-toe footwear

Hats & caps: Allowed only if they're company-issued and display your company logo.

Safety Reminders

Backroom and sales floor safety protects you, our associates, and our customers. The basics:

Carts

  • Don't overload U-boats or stack product above the end rails.
  • Never stand on a U-boat or use it as a step-stool.

Compactors & Dumpsters

  • Cardboard goes in the baler. Shrink wrap goes in the recycling bin or baler. Leave a clean work space.

Pallet Jacks

  • Non-powered pallet jacks are available. Confirm with the store manager before bringing one onto the sales floor during business hours.
  • You're responsible for your jack and any empty pallets while you're on the floor.

Spills

  • Clean up any spill you cause using absorbent. Mark the area with a caution sign until it's safe.

Step-Stools & Ladders

  • Use only the approved step-stools and ladders Fred Meyer provides — styles vary by division.
  • Ladder users must complete fall protection, ladder safety, and pre-use inspection training.
  • Never substitute milk crates or shelving for a step-stool.

  • Keep your work area clean and monitor every tool you bring onto the floor.
Questions or concerns? Talk to the store's Operations Assistant Store Manager during your visit, or call the ethics hotline at 1-800-689-4609. Thanks for representing your company — and ours — well.
