Dress & Conduct Policy

Conduct

BEFORE YOU START

Park in outlying lot areas. Enter through the back and sign the vendor log first thing.

Leave kids at home. Children are not permitted while you work, and only adults 18+ may operate backroom

equipment such as the cardboard baler.

WHILE YOU'RE ON THE FLOOR

Help customers when asked. If you can't answer, find a store associate — never reply with "I don't work

here."

No pallets on the sales floor after the division-mandated cutoff. Use U-boats or appropriate vendor carts, and
pull, don't push, for clear sightlines.

Shopping carts are for customers only. Don't use them to move or store product.

Reset team exception: SAS / Retail Odyssey has been granted exclusive rights by Kroger corporate to use

shopping carts during resets. This is standard corporate policy, but expect occasional pushback from store

employees who aren't aware of the exception. Two non-negotiables: never leave product in a cart in the

backroom, and empty and return every cart to the corral before ending your shift.

No music devices, headphones, or earbuds — anywhere in the store, anytime.

Cell phones stay on vibrate. Use them only for business needs, and never while moving product on the floor.

No eating, drinking, or tobacco use (including smokeless) on store premises.

You are not authorized to operate powered forklifts.

Wipe down shelves and display areas weekly, or more often as needed.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE

Don't leave trash or product on the floor. Distressed merchandise goes to the backroom for credit.

Combine like items on one pallet, stretch wrap for safety, and stage in the designated backroom area.

Heads up: Keep receipts for everything you purchase. Bags and briefcases may be searched. Suspect

something off? Call the ethics hotline at 1-800-689-4609.

V E N D O R STA N DA R D S

Thanks for partnering with us. These standards keep our stores safe, professional, and welcoming for

customers — and they apply to every vendor visit. Read it once, then carry the basics into every store

you service.

Dress Code

Show up clean, well-groomed, and identifiable. Wear a name badge that displays both your company name and
your own name.

SHIRTS

✓ ACCEPTABLE

Polo shirts

Blouses or shirts with sleeves

Sweaters and cardigans

Anything that covers the midriff

✕ NOT ACCEPTABLE

T-shirts and sweatshirts

Tank tops or spaghetti straps

Shirts with non–company/product graphics or text

SLACKS & SKIRTS

✓ ACCEPTABLE

Casual slacks (e.g., Dockers) worn at the waist

Company-issue uniform shorts

Clean, well-kept jeans (if your company allows them)

✕ NOT ACCEPTABLE

Sweatpants or exercise wear

Tight, cutoff, or baggy shorts

Anything shorter than 3 inches above the knee

SHOES

✓ ACCEPTABLE

Closed-toe casual or low-heel shoes

Boating or deck shoes

Steel-toe and athletic shoes

Loafers and dress shoes

✕ NOT ACCEPTABLE

Casual sandals

Flip-flops

Any open-toe footwear

Hats & caps: Allowed only if they're company-issued and display your company logo.

Safety Reminders

Backroom and sales floor safety protects you, our associates, and our customers. The basics:

CARTS

Don't overload U-boats or stack product above the end rails.

Never stand on a U-boat or use it as a step-stool.

COMPACTORS & DUMPSTERS

Cardboard goes in the baler. Shrink wrap goes in the recycling bin or baler. Leave a clean work space.

PALLET JACKS

Non-powered pallet jacks are available. Confirm with the store manager before bringing one onto the sales
floor during business hours.

You're responsible for your jack and any empty pallets while you're on the floor.

SPILLS

Clean up any spill you cause using absorbent. Mark the area with a caution sign until it's safe.

CAUTION

CONE

ABSORBENT

Keep Floors Clean

Cones and absorbent are stocked at every store. Use both — sign goes up first,

absorbent next, and the area stays marked until the floor is dry.

STEP-STOOLS & LADDERS

Use only the approved step-stools and ladders Fred Meyer provides — styles vary by division.

Ladder users must complete fall protection, ladder safety, and pre-use inspection training.

Never substitute milk crates or shelving for a step-stool.

✕ MILK CRATES
✕ SHELVING

Don't Improvise

Milk crates, shelves, U-boats, and any other workaround are off-limits as

substitutes for a step-stool — full stop. If a stool isn't available, ask a store

associate before going up.

STOCKING

Keep your work area clean and monitor every tool you bring onto the floor.

Questions or concerns? Talk to the store's Operations Assistant Store Manager during your visit, or call the ethics

hotline at 1-800-689-4609. Thanks for representing your company — and ours — well.
