Reset Procedures

The full reset workflow — from verification through cleanup. Each phase has its own standards. Don't skip ahead.

Phase 1 · Pre-Reset
Verify Before You Touch a Shelf
  • Verify you are working on the correct set and location — ask your Team Lead if unsure.
  • Review the POG and Kompass notes before starting.
  • Pull all discontinued items ("disco" items).
Phase 2 · During Reset
Execute the Set
  • Remove old shelf strips and install new strips as needed (you may not receive strips for the entire set).
  • Measure shelf heights on every set, every time.
  • Clean while resetting:
    • Full Updates — clean all shelves
    • NII / Maintenance Sets — clean open shelving only
  • Reset products per the POG, keeping the old tag with the product when moving items.
  • Set the correct number of facings.
Phase 3 · Tag Placement
The Tag Travels With the Product
  • Critical: at every stage of the reset, the tag travels with the product.
  • When moved to the shelf, place the tag flat on the LEFT side of the strip, covering the printed product info underneath.
  • Replace the tag only if the new tag has a newer date. If not, leave the old tag in place.
Phase 4 · Post-Reset
Close It Out Cleanly
  • Label and store all Not in Set / Backstock items correctly.
  • Check the Kompass cart for new items.
  • Clean the area: trash in bags, cardboard disposed of properly.
  • On the POG, highlight any items missing tags.
  • Return the highlighted POG to your Team Lead, who will pass it to the price changer to have the missing tags made.
  • Major issues → report to the Team Lead immediately. They'll raise concerns to the Supervisor.
  • Minor issues → communicate when returning the POG to the Team Lead.

Electronics & Personal Devices

Stay focused, stay accessible. The store floor is a customer-facing space.

✕ Not Allowed
  • Music-playing devices on the sales floor
  • Earbuds or headphones on the sales floor
  • Personal cell phone use during work
✓ Cell Phone Rules
  • Keep phones on silent at all times
  • Use only for business purposes
  • Never while moving product on the floor

Workplace Expectations

Professionalism

  • Maintain professional language and appropriate workplace conversations at all times.

Keeping Aisles Shoppable

  • If using multiple carts, move carts that aren't actively being worked to nearby aisles or main walkways.
  • Always leave space for customers.

Customer Assistance

  • Help when able.
  • If unable, politely find a Fred Meyer associate to assist.

Food, Drink & Tobacco

  • No eating or drinking on the sales floor or in the backroom.
  • No tobacco use on Fred Meyer property — including smokeless tobacco.
