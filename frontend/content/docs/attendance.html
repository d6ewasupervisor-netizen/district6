Team — showing up on time, every time, is the single most important commitment you make to the team. This document covers how we track time, what counts as on-time, and what happens when expectations aren't met.

Clocking In & Out

  • Clock in and out for every shift and lunch break using your PIN.
  • Do not share your PIN with anyone.

Waiving Lunch

If you choose to skip your lunch, send a text or email to your supervisor confirming you waived it. No confirmation = no waiver.

Time Off & PTO

⚠ Advance Approval Required

All time off must be requested and approved in advance. Submitting a request is not the same as receiving approval — do not assume your time off is covered until your supervisor confirms it. Unapproved absences are treated under the Attendance Escalation Process below.

Time Off Requests
  • Submit requests as far in advance as possible.
  • Wait for explicit confirmation from your supervisor.
  • Without confirmation, you are still scheduled.
Paid Time Off (PTO)
  • Part-Time — earns sick time
  • Full-Time — earns sick & vacation time
  • Balances and requests in Oracle

Attendance Expectations

From the Employee Handbook. The standard for every shift, every week.

  • Arrive on time for your shift.
  • Stay for your entire scheduled work time.
  • If you cannot come to work:
    • Call your supervisor as soon as possible.
    • Call must be made no later than 2 hours before your scheduled start time.
    • Call in each day you are scheduled but unable to work.
⚠ Critical Rule

Contact your direct Supervisor — NOT your Team Lead. Team Leads cannot approve absences or document call-outs.

Attendance Escalation Process

Excessive absences or tardiness may result in discipline, up to and including termination. The progression depends on the nature of the violation.

No Call / No Show

A No Call / No Show means you did not come to work and did not notify your supervisor.

Tardiness or Leaving Early Without Approval

What Counts as Tardy

You must be in the store, checked in with your Team Lead, and ready to work at your scheduled start time. Tardiness begins 5 minutes after your scheduled start time.

Additional Triggers

Failure to give proper notice when you cannot work or need to leave early results in an immediate write-up if supervisor approval was not received. Leaving before work is completed without permission is treated as job abandonment and may result in termination.

Reset Window

The offense counter resets after 12 months with no incidents. Stay clean for a year and the slate clears.
