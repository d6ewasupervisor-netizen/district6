Time Off & PTO

⚠ ADVANCE APPROVAL REQUIRED

All time off must be requested and approved in advance. Submitting a request is not the same as receiving approval

— do not assume your time off is covered until your supervisor confirms it. Unapproved absences are treated under

the Attendance Escalation Process below.

TIME OFF REQUESTS

Submit requests as far in advance as possible.

Wait for explicit confirmation from your supervisor.

Without confirmation, you are still scheduled.

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

Part-Time — earns sick time

Full-Time — earns sick & vacation time

Balances and requests in Oracle

Attendance Expectations

From the Employee Handbook. The standard for every shift, every week.

Arrive on time for your shift.

Stay for your entire scheduled work time.

If you cannot come to work:

Call your supervisor as soon as possible.

Call must be made no later than 2 hours before your scheduled start time.

Call in each day you are scheduled but unable to work.

⚠ CRITICAL RULE

Contact your direct Supervisor — NOT your Team Lead. Team Leads cannot approve absences or document call-outs.

Attendance Escalation Process

Excessive absences or tardiness may result in discipline, up to and including termination. The progression depends on the

nature of the violation.

NO CALL / NO SHOW

TARDINESS OR LEAVING EARLY WITHOUT APPROVAL

WHAT COUNTS AS TARDY

You must be in the store, checked in with your Team Lead, and ready to work at your scheduled start time. Tardiness

begins 5 minutes after your scheduled start time.

1
Attendance policy reminder

2
Verbal warning

3
Written warning

4
Final written warning

5
Termination

ADDITIONAL TRIGGERS

Failure to give proper notice when you cannot work or need to leave early results in an immediate write-up if

supervisor approval was not received. Leaving before work is completed without permission is treated as job

abandonment and may result in termination.

RESET WINDOW

The offense counter resets after 12 months with no incidents. Stay clean for a year and the slate clears.
