Dress Code Policy

|  S P R I N G  2 0 2 6  E D I T I O N
Effective May 1, 2026  ·  Owner: April Gauthier, Supervisor, Eastern Washington
Team — our goal is to maintain a casual, professional, and safe work environment. How you show up reflects on you, on the company, and on the standard our customers expect to see in store.

General Expectations

  • Dress should be neat, clean, and professional.
  • Practice good grooming and hygiene.
  • Clothing, hair, jewelry, and makeup must be appropriate for work.
  • Always use good judgment when choosing what to wear.
  • Presentation must be safe for the work environment.

Attire

✓ Acceptable
  • Polo shirts
  • T-shirts with a company logo
  • Casual slacks
  • Clean, non-ripped jeans
  • Close-toed shoes
  • Required name badge with your name and company name
  • Clean, well-groomed appearance at all times
✕ Not Acceptable
Clothing
  • Overalls, leggings, shorts, mini-skirts
  • Sweatshirts, sweatpants, exercise or athletic wear
  • Tank tops, spaghetti-strap shirts
  • Cropped tops, midriff-baring shirts, low-back tops
  • Beachwear or sleepwear
  • Non–company/product graphics or text
  • Dirty, wrinkled, torn, ripped, sheer, revealing, or ill-fitting clothing

Shoes
  • Sandals, flip-flops, slippers, or any open-toed footwear
Accessories & Appearance
  • Jewelry, accessories, or hairstyles that create a safety hazard
  • Excessive or distracting jewelry or accessories
  • Strong or excessive odors — heavy perfume, cologne, smoke, or body odor
  • Tattoos with offensive imagery, language, or symbols must be covered
⚠ Coverage Requirement

Shirts must fully cover the midriff, lower back, and shoulders at all times.

Electronics & Personal Devices

Stay focused, stay accessible. The store floor is a customer-facing space.

✕ Not Allowed
  • Music-playing devices (speakers) on the sales floor
  • Earbuds or headphones on the sales floor
  • Personal cell phone use during work
✓ Cell Phone Rules
  • Keep phones on silent at all times
  • Use only for business purposes
Final Reminder

If you are unsure whether something is appropriate, ask your supervisor before your shift. Everyone plays a role in maintaining a safe, professional, and welcoming workplace.
