Store Responsibilities

The store designates a Kompass Champion — typically the Operations Assistant Store Manager — who is
scheduled to work on Kompass Day and owns the prep, support, and sign-off workflow below.

Reality check: A dedicated Kompass Champion is rarely staffed in stores today. The majority of these

responsibilities have shifted onto the Retail Odyssey Team and Lead, who absorb the prep, organization, and

sign-off coordination in the Champion's absence. The list below remains the formal store standard — use it to set

expectations during the walk and sign-off.

BEFORE THE VISIT

Print and file Kompass Notes for the upcoming week.

Coordinate with the Price Changer so all Kompass tags are filed prior to Kompass Day.

Locate and secure the Vestcom box (FedEx tracking is provided weekly).

Open the Vestcom box and confirm planograms and strips are correct — verify set sizes (3' vs. 4' segments,

etc.).

Email the Kompass Managers or Kompass Merchandising Support with any planogram discrepancies

before Kompass Day.

KOMPASS CART SETUP

Keep the Kompass cart organized and accessible to the team.

Only new items for Kompass belong on the cart — reference Kompass Notes for any discrepancies.

Stock cleaning supplies and have them ready for the team.

Provide a clearly designated location for perishable and frozen new items.

KO M PA S S P R O G R A M

KOMPASS — Kroger Optimization Management Plan Aligning Store Sales.

A clear split of duties between the store and the Retail Odyssey Kompass

Team so every weekly visit runs smoothly, on time, and to standard.

WALK-THROUGH & SIGN-OFF

Walk every set with the Kompass Team upon completion.

Ensure missing or incorrect strips have been communicated to the Kompass Managers.

Provide signature to the Team Lead approving the weekly work — completed before the team leaves the

store.

Sign off all work in Kompass Completion by the end of each visit, using the sign-off sheets provided by the

Team Lead.

Store-only access: Kompass Reps are not permitted to access the CAO website or the Kompass Completion

site. Sign-off in those systems is a store responsibility.

Kompass Team Responsibilities

CHECK-IN

Check in with the Kompass Champion or Food Manager by the predetermined start time.

Coordinate with the store on early entry as needed.

Pull Kompass Notes, planograms, strips, and tags from the Kompass cart.

For issues or discrepancies, the Team Lead contacts the respective Retail Odyssey District Supervisor.

Team Lead assigns each team member to a project.

RESET TYPES

FULL RESET

Kompass Updates

Maintain a clean, shopper-friendly aisle throughout

the project. Keep materials, baskets, and carts on

one side to leave a clear customer pathway.

Pull all DI's per Kompass Notes (designated with

closeout tags) and route to the store associate or

place in clearly labeled boxes.

Pull existing strips from the set; install new strips per

the notes.

Set the category to the new planogram.

Rotate older stock to the front, newer stock to the

back of each facing — proper Key Retailing rotation.

Pull all new-item stock from the back room onto the

shelf.

Wipe down all moved shelving and tag moldings.

Hang all tags provided, or hand-write tags for the

store to recreate.

Move backstock to the designated associate or

labeled overstock boxes.

PARTIAL

NIIs & Maintenances

Maintain a clean, shopper-friendly aisle throughout.

Pull all DI's per Kompass Notes (designated with

closeout tags) and route to the store associate or

place in clearly labeled boxes.

Replace existing strips with the new strips ordered

for the changed shelves only — not the entire set.

Set the changing area of the planogram to the new

strips.

For categories without strips, cut new items into the

set per the planogram.

Wipe down only the affected shelves — not the entire

set.

Hang all tags provided, or hand-write tags for the

store to recreate.

Move backstock to the designated associate or

labeled overstock boxes.

COMPLETING THE WEEKLY PROCESS

1
Notify the Champion

Contact the Kompass Champion or Food Manager with an estimated completion time.

2
Fill out sign-off sheets

Complete a sheet for the store and a Summary Sign-Off sheet for the Champion or Food Manager to sign.

3
Capture the signature

By signing, the store agrees that all work is completed to standard. Take a photo of the signed Summary page.

4
Walk every project with the store

Show the Champion the location of all overstock and discontinued items.

5
Hand off and confirm

Leave a copy of the completed sign-off sheets for the store's records and confirm sign-off in Kompass Completion is the
store's responsibility.

Questions or planogram discrepancies? Email the Kompass Managers or Kompass Merchandising Support

before Kompass Day. For day-of issues, the Team Lead routes through the Retail Odyssey District Supervisor.
